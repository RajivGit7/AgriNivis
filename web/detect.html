Your result will appear here

Select a leaf photo and press Analyse. You will get the most likely disease, the model's confidence, and a treatment checklist.

Use natural light

Avoid harsh shadows, flash glare and heavily filtered photos. Daylight usually gives the model the clearest colour signal.

Fill the frame

Place a single leaf on a plain surface and keep the affected area sharp. Avoid mixing several leaves or background clutter.

Check the confidence

Below 60% the result is flagged as uncertain. Take another photo or ask an agronomist before acting on a low-confidence label.

This is not a laboratory test. The model recognises only the 12 classes it was trained on. A confident prediction can still be wrong, and an unknown disease may be forced into one of those classes. Confirm with your local extension service before spraying.

Model API URL

AgriNivis keeps the ML model on a separate service. Enter the public URL of your deployed inference API, for example https://username-agrinivis-ml.hf.space. Leave blank to use the value in assets/js/config.js.
