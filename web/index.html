How it works

Three steps from uncertainty to action.

No account, no complicated setup. Start with a photo or a short field questionnaire and get an answer you can use the same day.

Capture or describe

Take a clear photo of a single affected leaf, or enter the basic conditions of your plot in the recommendation form.

Let the model analyse

The TFLite vision model runs on a lightweight inference service. The recommender applies agronomic rules plus live weather data.

Act with context

Read the likely diagnosis, confidence and treatment guidance, or compare ranked crops with the reasons behind each score.

Act early

Early detection limits spread. The result includes practical prevention and treatment steps, not just a label.

Use local context

Recommendations combine field conditions with temperature and rainfall from a free weather service, using your coordinates.

Built to be clear

Every score is explainable. Every prediction carries a confidence value and a reminder to confirm with a local agronomist.

AgriNivis is a screening aid. It does not replace laboratory diagnosis or a licensed agronomist. Always confirm a disease before applying pesticides, and follow the product label and local regulations.
